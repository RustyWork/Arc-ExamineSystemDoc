# 🧱 Basic Setup

---

After importing the <span style={{color: '#ff8011'}}>**Arc Examine System**</span>, you will find a folder in your project tab under the Assets folder.

![Alt text](\img\109.png)

<br />

In the <span style={{color: '#ff8011'}}>**Arc Examine System**</span> folder, you will be able to find Demo, scripts, and prefabs, etc.

<br />

Go into the **prefabs** folder, and you will find the following prefabs:

- <span style={{color: '#ff8011'}}>**ExamineManager**</span>
- <span style={{color: '#ff8011'}}>**ExamineManager-UI**</span>

Drag them into your scene to start your setup.

<br />

Also add a <span style={{color: '#ff8011'}}>**Event System**</span> in scene to avoid further complications.

<br />

:::note

And there should be a layer named **Examinable**, it is added automatically on importing the asset.

:::

<br />

If **Examinable** layer got deleted, look on top, you will find a tab named **Arc ExamineSystem**, and after clicking on it, click on **Wizard**.

A small window will open. Click on **Setup**, and the layer will be added.
